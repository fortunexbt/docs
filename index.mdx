<section className="fm-index fm-index-notes" aria-labelledby="labels">
  <h2 className="fm-index-group" id="labels">What the labels mean</h2>
  <dl className="fm-labels">
    <dt>Shipped</dt><dd>A release or public use path exists. It does not mean every deployment is supported.</dd>
    <dt>Verified</dt><dd>A reproducible check or inspectable artifact backs the claim, not a badge that passed once.</dd>
    <dt>Experiment</dt><dd>Real code runs inside a deliberately limited envelope. It is not production-ready.</dd>
    <dt>Simulated</dt><dd>The behaviour is mocked or emulated on purpose and is not connected to the named external system.</dd>
    <dt>Planned</dt><dd>The direction is written down and not implemented.</dd>
  </dl>
  <p className="fm-index-prose">A label attaches to a specific surface, such as a release, a hosted demo or a command, not to a whole project.</p>
